The heart of this project

Curiosity without judgment.

Mental health is part of being human. Diagnoses can help describe patterns and guide care, but they never tell someone’s whole story. This project aims to make learning feel approachable while treating real people and experiences with respect.

01Listen first. Lived experience is personal; don’t assume one story represents everyone.
02Use kind language. Ask people how they want to be described.
03Stay curious. Check reliable sources and be willing to learn more.

A better starting point

Make room for nuance.

Simple assumptions can get in the way of understanding. Here are a few reminders for thoughtful conversations.

↗

One label, many lives

Remember: People with the same diagnosis may have very different experiences, strengths, and support needs.

♡

Support is personal

Remember: There is no single right response. Listening and asking what would help can matter.

✦

A person is more

Remember: A diagnosis is one part of someone’s life—not their identity, future, or value.
